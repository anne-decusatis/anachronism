In this post I count the number of competitors and stated genders of combatants of East Kingdom Crown Tournaments from 2022-2024, to compare with the rapier crown tournament held Spring 2025. 

I gathered this data by hand from the East Kingdom wiki and facebook. It took me about 2 hours to gather the pronouns and another about half hour to find the lists of combatants (searching facebook, EK Seneschal Blog, EK seneschal blog archive, EK announcement archive).

Corrections are welcome, my stats knowledge is rusty now. 

## Summary statistics

## Did more people compete in the Rapier Crown Tournament than usual?

Average combatants per crown tournament: 32.3 (24, 23, 40, 33, 40, 34)

Null hypothesis: The 88 combatants in the rapier crown tournament is within normal variance of the average number of combatants in East Kingdom Crown Tournament

Alternative hypothesis: More people signed up for the rapier crown tournament than recent armored combat tournaments.

The t-value is -18.308586. The value of p is < .00001. The result is significant at p < .05.
<https://www.socscistatistics.com/tests/tsinglesample/default2.aspx>

There is a statistically significant difference between the number of combatants in the rapier crown tournament than in the armored combat crown tournaments. (we reject the null hypothesis)

### Did more non-he/him pronoun users compete in the Rapier Crown Tournament than usual for a crown tournament?

Data points for armored combat tournaments: 18.8, 8.3, 20.8, 13.0, 12.5, 18.8

For the rapier tournament it was 45%.


Null hypothesis: The 45% non-he/him combatants in the rapier crown tournament is within normal variance of the average number of non-he/him combatants in East Kingdom Crown Tournament

Alternative hypothesis: More non he/him combatants signed up for the rapier crown tournament than recent armored combat tournaments.

The t-value is -15.014836. The value of p is .000024. The result is significant at p < .05.
<https://www.socscistatistics.com/tests/tsinglesample/default2.aspx>

There is a statistically significant difference between the number of non he/him combatants in the rapier crown tournament than in the armored combat crown tournaments. (we reject the null hypothesis)
