I was sad to not be able to attend today's tournament in person (all hail the crown prince & princess!) but the livestream gave me some time to do other things on the side and write this post. 

Here's what I think so far:

* It definitely increased the number of competitors and the event size
* I really enjoyed seeing people I've known and fought competing on the crown list field. (I was rooting for Bobby and Yin to win, or one of the Tadcasters -- I've had wonderful personal experiences with both at Pennsics past)
* The final 16 still did not include anyone using she/her pronouns
* This tournament is not a substitute for truly inclusve governance and incident handling that is entirely out-of-game
* To do: count number of competitors and stated genders of combatants of East Kingdom Crown Tournaments from 2022 onwards, to compare

## Methodology

You can't know if someone is a gender minority by looking at their pronouns, you can only know by asking them. Grouping "gender diverse" combatants as people using non-he/him pronouns misses binary trans men and people who are nonbinary but use he/him pronouns, so this gender diversity calculation based on pronouns here is likely a floor for the actual metric, rather than the entire truth.

I welcome corrections from those in the SCA Rapier community, with two caveats: 
1. I will only be accepting pronoun corrections conveyed to me by the individuals themselves, whether via publicly available materials or personal communications. 
2. I understand that it may out people who don't want to be outed for me to post their info on my website, and so I may not update this post, even if I privately receive information that it is incorrect. I request that readers of my website take this listing with a grain of salt particularly as time passes and public identities may change.

## Summary statistics on pronoun usage as stated on East Kingdom Wiki for spring 2025 combatants

* She/her: 22 (25%)
* He/him: 34 (39%)
* They/them: 2 (3%)
* She/they: 2 (3%)
* He/they: 2 (3%)
* No data: 26 (30%)
Total: 88 (100%)

Declared as not he/him: 28 of 62 (45%)

* People who listed their pronouns on their wiki: 19 (22%)
* People who were referred to in the third person on their wiki, from which I inferred pronouns, assuming that wiki pages are authored by the people they're about on the East Kingdom Wiki: 43 (49%)
* No pronoun data on wiki: 26 (30%)
Total: 88 (100%)

On a personal note, I often find in mundane life that cisgender men don't bother to list their pronouns, so I was heartened by the large number (7) of people with he/him pronouns declared on the East Kingdom Wiki. Thank you for your support for sharing pronouns! I feel it makes it easier for me to share my non-standard pronouns when it is commonplace!
